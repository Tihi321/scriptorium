## Open: findings of the hub's HUB-04 live check (2026-10-22)

A book (`the-umbrella-conductor`, a short story) was written over MCP on the master build a8cf243 (SCP-14 and SCP-19). It published; these are what it found. Not fixed yet. Fix each on its own branch `SCP-NN_<what>` cut from `master`. The causes are read from the code, not reproduced.

- **SCP-20: the title changed although the story fixed it.** The story brief had `title_fixed: true` and the book first had that title, but the published book was called "The Yellow Umbrella" (the title of the chapter); `book.md` and `get_book` showed it, and `rename_book` restored it. Cause: the publisher job (`handlers.ts` `publish`) sets `title` from the publisher's answer (`r.value.title ... || book.title`) and never looks at `title_fixed` in the book's `brief.md`. A one-chapter book probably invites the publisher to take the chapter title. Fix: keep the book's title when the brief says `title_fixed` (and tell the publisher the title is fixed); a test with a fixed title and a publisher that answers another one.
- **SCP-21: the story year is guessed from a date in the text.** The rewrite prompts said "The story is set in 1989 (from the book's own files)", although the outline's `story_year` was `null` and `get_book.year` was null; the 1989 came from a date on a tram ticket in the text. The read-aloud reviewer then reported a "timeline contradiction", which cost a round. Cause: `calendar.ts` `storyYear` falls back to the year that the book's texts name most often when `story_year` is not a number, and `calendarSection` states it as fact ("from the book's own files"). The outline schema also accepts `story_year: null` for a story that has no year (a bedtime story), which is the case that then gets a guessed one. Fix: when the architect said `null` (no fixed year) do not guess from the texts, or say "not stated" in the section; word the guessed case as a guess; a test with a null `story_year` and a year in the text.
- **SCP-22: `log.md` interleaves the parallel reviewer calls.** The book's `log.md` had fragments of several reviewers' calls mixed together (the files in `reviews/` were clean). Cause: `logs/logs.ts` `LogWriter.append` buffers text per file and writes it every 250 ms, and every agent's streamed chunks go to the same book log (`both(agent, book, text)`) without a line or block of its own, so parallel calls mix. Fix: write a whole call (or whole lines with the agent's id) at a time, or give each call its own block; a test with two parallel appends.
- **SCP-23: the pitch limit of 300 words is not enforced.** A pitch of 315 words was accepted. Cause: `prompts/architect/pitch.md` only asks "Keep it under 300 words"; the pitch job (`handlers.ts`, the `pitch` job) does not count. Fix: one tighten or re-ask when the pitch is over the limit by more than a few percent (like the chapter tighten), or drop the number from the prompt if it is not meant to bind.
- **SCP-24: research asks for real-world facts for a bedtime story.** `research_plan` and `research_prep` ask the researcher for real-world facts even for a bedtime story. Harmless with an empty list (nothing is searched, nothing is spent), but a wasted model call. Cause: `advance.ts` always plans research for a book that is not an adaptation (`research_plan`, one architect call; the architect then decides whether anything is needed), and the writer's prep (`research_prep`) runs per chapter for formats or genres in `research_prep_genres`. Fix: skip the plan call for formats that need no facts (juvenile read-aloud formats such as `bedtime-*`), and the prep for them; a test that a bedtime book makes no `research_plan` job.
- **SCP-25: a second packaged instance exits silently.** Hub-side observation: a packaged `Scriptorium.exe` (`dist\win-unpacked`) left open held Electron's single-instance lock with its MCP off, so a headless start (`yarn headless` or the exe with `--mcp`) exited without a message; `err.log` had only a Chromium cache warning. Cause: `src/main/index.ts` `if (!app.requestSingleInstanceLock()) app.quit()` quits at once and says nothing. Fix: write "another Scriptorium is running (pid N, data folder X); this start was not needed" to the log and stderr before quitting, and, when the running one has MCP off while this start asked for it, say so. (The engine lock `index/engine.lock` is another lock: it holds a pid and start time and `acquireEngineLock` throws when a live process has it.)
