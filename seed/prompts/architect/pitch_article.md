Write the pitch in plain text with these parts: "Headline:" (one line, specific, no clickbait, under 14 words), "Standfirst:" (one or two sentences that tell the reader what happened or what the piece explains and why it matters), "Angle:" (what this article says that a plain news summary does not: the question it answers, the view it takes or the person or place it follows), "Structure:" (the main points in order, one line each), and "Sources to find:" (the kinds of recent sources it needs: who has to be quoted or cited, what documents, reports or data). Only real, checkable subject matter: do not invent people, events, studies, quotes or numbers, and do not state a date or figure you cannot source. If the idea names something you do not know is real, say what must be checked first. Keep it under 300 words.
